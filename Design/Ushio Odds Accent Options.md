# Ushio Odds Accent Options

Proposals only. No brand selection has been finalised. Neutral surfaces and semantic status colours remain as specified in the initial palette.

| Accent | Light | Dark | Light button contrast | Dark button contrast |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 / Cobalt | #1D4ED8 | #93C5FD | 6.7:1 | 10.47:1 |
| 02 / Violet | #6D28D9 | #C4B5FD | 7.1:1 | 10.23:1 |
| 03 / Burnt orange | #C2410C | #FDBA74 | 5.18:1 | 11.2:1 |
| 04 / Monochrome | #171717 | #FAFAFA | 17.93:1 | 18.09:1 |
| 05 / Ocean teal | #0F766E | #5EEAD4 | 5.47:1 | 12.76:1 |

Recommended shortlist: cobalt for a professional terminal, violet for a more distinctive identity, monochrome for the quietest interface. Burnt orange needs extra separation from amber warnings. All ratios are calculated using WCAG relative luminance; they do not establish whole-interface accessibility.
