Ushio Odds / Brand foundations / 01

Quiet surfaces. Clear signals.

A neutral terminal palette with an ocean-teal signature. Colour guides attention to a price, an action or a change in status.

01 / Mostly neutral

Black, white and grey carry navigation, tables and surfaces. Keep teal on a few high-value elements. No coloured page backgrounds or decorative gradients.

02 / Separate meaning

Teal means selected or actionable. Green means a positive calculated result. Amber means caution. Red means an error or invalid quote. Labels and symbols accompany colour.

03 / Readable at speed

Every specified body-text pair and accent-button pair exceeds 4.5:1 contrast. Use aligned tabular numbers, visible focus rings and quiet borders. Borders alone are not control boundaries.
